Handing a block theme to a client

21 Feb 2026

A block theme hands the client more than a set of pages: it hands them the styling controls too. The handover question is which of those controls to leave open.

What editors reliably break is the type scale and the colour palette — not out of carelessness, but because the controls invite a one-off exception, and the exception outlives the reason for it. Locking those in theme.json, and leaving content, media and order open, keeps the design intact without making the site read-only.

Patterns do the rest of the work. A section that exists as a pattern is one an editor can add and rearrange; a section that exists only in a template is one they cannot get wrong. Deciding which sections belong in which group is most of the handover.

The last step is the least technical: write down what was locked and why, so the next person does not unlock it by accident.
